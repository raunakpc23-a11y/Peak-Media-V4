Privacy & Data Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026 · Peak Media (Film, Series & Music Vault)

Peak Media values your privacy. We collect minimal data solely to operate optional cloud sync, keep playback and interface features responsive, and improve recommendations across movies, TV series, and music.

1. Data Stored Locally vs. Cloud Sync

  • Guest Mode (Local Only): Your watchlist, watched titles, ratings, favorites, theme preferences, and custom lists stay in your browser's local storage.
  • Optional Account & Cloud Sync: If you choose to create an account or sign in via Supabase, we store your chosen username, hashed password, optional recovery email, and synced library snapshot so you can restore your list across devices.

2. Operational Metrics We Record

  • Device Specifications: Browser platform and viewport dimensions for responsive layout support.
  • General Region: Coarse city/timezone information for locale and catalog formatting.
  • Session Duration & Media Interactions: Aggregated playback, search, and watchlist actions used inside the app telemetry log.

3. Data Security & No Third-Party Tracking

Cloud database tables use PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS) and salted password hashing on Supabase. Peak Media does not sell personal information, share data with data brokers, or run third-party advertising trackers.

4. Exporting & Deleting Your Data

You can export your complete library backup anytime from the Profile tab in JSON format, clear your local browser storage, or sign out to revoke active session tokens. For repository or data deletion inquiries, contact the maintainer via the Peak Media repository at github.com/raunakpc23-a11y/Peak-Media-V4.
